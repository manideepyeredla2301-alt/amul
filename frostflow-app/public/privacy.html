Mr Enterprises Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Mr Enterprises uses customer information in FrostFlow and its WhatsApp business communications. FrostFlow supports distribution and retail sales, customer accounts, orders, invoices and payment follow-up.

Information processed

We process contact details such as customer names, telephone numbers and delivery addresses; retailer and route details; order and invoice records; payment amounts, references and balances; and tax identifiers where provided for billing. WhatsApp records may include your profile name, messages, button or list selections, attachment metadata, message identifiers, timestamps, and delivery or failure information.

How information is used

Information is used to handle enquiries, prepare and fulfil orders, maintain customer accounts, issue invoices, record payments, send transaction updates and payment reminders, and investigate messaging or billing problems.

Storage and service providers

FrostFlow business records and received WhatsApp events are stored in a database on the business computer. Backup copies may also contain these records. WhatsApp communications are processed by Meta through the WhatsApp Business Platform. A hosting or HTTPS tunnel provider routes webhook traffic to the business computer and may process message data in transit. Meta and those providers process information under their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

Retention

The current application does not automatically delete customer records or WhatsApp messages after a fixed period. Removal is handled by the business. Some transaction records may need to be retained for accounting, tax, dispute resolution or other applicable requirements.

Contact, corrections and deletion requests

Contact Mr Enterprises at +91 9014003991 on WhatsApp to request access to or correction of your customer details, ask about deletion, or request that WhatsApp reminders stop. Please identify the relevant customer account or invoice. Requests require manual review; sending a message does not automatically delete records or stop reminders. We may need to verify your identity and explain any records that must be retained.

Security

The webhook receiver checks Meta's request signature before accepting events. Access to the business computer, its database and backups must be restricted to authorised users. Please do not send passwords, payment PINs, OTPs or complete payment-card details through WhatsApp.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with an effective date.
